# Worked example - 1,400 sq m office floor (Group D)

For plumbing fixture counting, a Group D occupancy uses **14 sq m per person** (Sentence 3.7.4.2.(1)) - not the 9.30 sq m per person used for exiting.

- Occupant load for fixtures: 1,400 / 14 = 100 persons, taken as 50 of each sex.
- Table 3.7.4.7.: 50 persons of each sex falls in the 50-74 row: **4 water closets for each sex**.
- **Total: 8 water closets.**

Reproduce it: `python3 washroom_fixtures.py --mode business --area-sqm 1400`
